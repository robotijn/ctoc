# Find recipes by ingredient

## Problem Statement

Home cooks often start from what is already in the fridge, not from a dish name. Today the
recipe site can only be searched by recipe title, so a cook with leeks and potatoes must
guess dish names until one matches. In a survey of 400 registered users, 61 percent said
they want to search by the ingredients they have.

## Target Audience

Registered home cooks who search the recipe site on a phone or a computer.

## Success Criteria

- A cook can type up to five ingredients and get recipes that use all of them, within
  2 seconds for 95 percent of searches.
- A search with no matching recipe tells the cook so and suggests removing one ingredient.

## Scope

In scope: searching published recipes by ingredient names in English, on the existing
search page. Out of scope: searching by dish name (already exists), dietary filters, and
ingredient names in other languages.
