# Habit tracker premium plan

## Problem Statement

People who track habits on more than one device lose their streaks: the free app keeps
habits on one phone only, so a user who switches to a tablet or a new phone starts from
zero. In the last quarter 1,200 support requests asked for sync between devices. We will
offer sync as part of a paid premium plan that a user subscribes to from inside the app.

## Target Audience

Existing free users of the habit tracker who use it on two or more devices.

## Success Criteria

- A free user can subscribe to premium from inside the app without contacting support.
- A premium user sees the same habits and streaks on every device they sign in on.

## Scope

In scope: subscribing to premium in the app, and syncing habits for premium users.
Out of scope: team or family plans, and the web version of the app.
