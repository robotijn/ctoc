# Faster recipe pages

## Problem Statement

Home cooks open a recipe on their phone while standing in the kitchen, often on a weak
mobile connection. Our recipe pages load the full photo gallery, the comments and the
video player before the ingredient list appears, so the cook waits with nothing to read.
Analytics show that 38 percent of mobile visitors leave a recipe page before it finishes
loading, and support mail names slowness as the most common complaint.

## Target Audience

Home cooks reading a recipe on a phone, usually over a mobile data connection.

## Success Criteria

- Fewer mobile visitors leave a recipe page before they can read it.
- A cook can start reading the ingredient list without waiting for photos, comments or video.

## Scope

In scope: the recipe page as seen on a phone. Out of scope: the desktop layout, the
search page, and the recipe editor used by authors.
