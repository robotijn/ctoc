# Saved recipes

## Problem Statement

Registered cooks find a recipe they want to make later and then cannot find it again: the
site has no way to keep a recipe, so they copy links into notes or search again from scratch.
In a survey of 400 registered users, 54 percent said they lost a recipe they meant to cook.

## Target Audience

Registered home cooks who browse the recipe site on a phone or a computer.

## Success Criteria

- A signed-in cook can save a recipe in one action and find it again in a "Saved" list.
- A cook can remove a recipe from the "Saved" list.

## Scope

In scope: saving, listing and removing saved recipes for signed-in cooks. Out of scope:
sharing saved lists, folders or tags, and saving recipes without an account.
