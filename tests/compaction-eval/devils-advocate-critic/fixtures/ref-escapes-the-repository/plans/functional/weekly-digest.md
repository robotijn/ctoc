# Weekly digest email

## What the person gets

Every Monday at 08:00 in their own time zone, a person receives one email listing the documents
shared with them during the previous seven days.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A person with at least one document shared with them in the previous seven days receives
   exactly one digest email on Monday between 08:00 and 08:15 in their own time zone.
2. A person with no newly shared documents receives no email.
3. Each listed document links to that document, and the link opens it for that person.
