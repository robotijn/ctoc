## Vision: Garden share

### Problem

Gardeners in one town have unused garden space while people on the allotment waiting list wait
three years for a plot. The two never meet, because the only matching happens by word of mouth.

### Target audience

Garden owners in one town with space they do not use, and people on the town's allotment waiting
list who want to grow vegetables.

### Success criteria

1. A garden owner offers a plot, with size and access times, in under five minutes.
2. A grower on the waiting list finds and requests a plot within one kilometre in under five minutes.
3. Within six months, 50 plots are shared.

### Scope

In: offering a plot; searching and requesting a plot; the owner accepting or declining a request.

Out: payments, insurance, tool lending, a mobile app.
