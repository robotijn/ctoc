## Vision: Loyalty boost

A points programme for the online shop.

### Outcome

Repeat orders grow by 30 percent within six months of launch.

### Success criteria

1. Repeat orders grow by 30 percent within six months of launch.
2. Points are redeemed on at least 40 percent of the orders that could redeem them.
3. The points balance page loads in under one second.

### Scope

In: earning points on every order, redeeming points at checkout, a points balance page.

Out: partner rewards, paid membership tiers, a mobile app.
