## Vision: Running club

One place where club members find and sign up for group runs and keep their own monthly distance.

### Problem

Members of a city running club miss group runs because the week's schedule is buried in a busy chat
thread, and they keep their own distances in spreadsheets they stop updating after a few weeks.

### Target audience

Members of one running club of about 200 runners, who run with the club once or twice a week and
open the chat on their phones. Each group run has a run leader, a member who posts the run and
counts who came.

### Success criteria

1. A member finds this week's group runs, with place and start time, in under 30 seconds.
2. At least half of the members who attend a group run, as counted by its run leader, sign up for it
   beforehand.
3. A member logs a run in under 20 seconds and sees their distance for the month.
4. At least 60 percent of active members (members who came to at least one group run that month) log a
   run in that month.

### Scope

In: a weekly list of group runs, posted by run leaders, with sign-up and an attendance count; logging a run's distance and date; a monthly
distance total per member.

Out: GPS tracking, race entries, payments, coaching plans, connections to running watches.
