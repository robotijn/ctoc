## Vision: Bookshop sales insights

### Problem

Owners of independent bookshops already have their sales: the till exports them every week as a
CSV file. Nobody reads those files, so owners reorder by memory and run out of their bestsellers
while slow sellers sit on the shelf.

### Target audience

Owners of independent bookshops with one to three shops whose till exports weekly sales as CSV.

### Success criteria

1. An owner imports a week of till sales from the CSV export in under two minutes.
2. An owner opens a monthly report of bestsellers and slow sellers, built from the sales they
   imported, and reorders from it in under ten minutes.

### Scope

In: importing the till's weekly CSV export; a monthly bestseller and slow-seller report built from
the imported sales; a reorder list drawn from the report.

Out: live connections to tills, ordering from suppliers, more than one currency.
